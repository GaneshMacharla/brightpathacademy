Structured Curriculum

The 8-Level Pathway to Mental Math Mastery

Every child progresses at their own pace with rigorous level assessments (80% pass criteria) and recognized certification at every milestone.

Level 1

Soroban Foundations

Pencil grip, abacus anatomy, bead values, simple direct 1-digit addition & subtraction.

~3 Months
Level 2

Small Friends Formulas

Complementary formula 5 (+4, +3, +2, +1), 2-digit sums, mental flash visualization.

~3 Months
Level 3

Big Friends Formulas

Complementary base 10 addition and subtraction, rapid 2-digit 3-row calculations.

~3 Months
Level 4

Multiplication Debut

2-digit by 1-digit multiplication, complex mixed formulas, speed Anzan drills.

~3 Months
Level 5

Division Mastery

Multi-digit division on Soroban, decimal numbers introduction, 3-digit multiplication.

~3 Months
Level 6

Decimal Operations

Addition and subtraction with decimals, percentage formulas, 4-digit sums.

~3 Months
Level 7

Roots & Advanced Anzan

Square roots, fractions, negative arithmetic, audio flash mental sums.

~3 Months
Level 8 (Grandmaster)

Grand Master Graduate

Complete mental calculation sovereignty, lightning competition speed, national certification.
