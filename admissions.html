How to Enroll in 4 Easy Steps

We ensure your child begins at the exact right difficulty level for maximum growth and zero stress.

1

WhatsApp Consultation

Send us a message on WhatsApp. Share your child's age, interest (Phonics, Abacus, Vedic Maths, Art or Tuitions), and grade to check slot availability.

2

Attend a free 45-minute interactive demo at our Pragati Nagar center or via live video call. Our faculty conducts a friendly baseline assessment.

3

Batch Selection

Choose your preferred slot (after-school weekday evening or Saturday/Sunday weekend batch, online or offline) based on your family schedule.

4

Student Kit & Launch

Receive the student curriculum workbooks, learning tools (Soroban abacus, art sheets, phonics guides) and start the exciting journey!
