Soroban Abacus: 8-Level Detailed Syllabus

Target Age: 4 to 14 Years | Batch Size: Max 8 Students | Mode: Classroom & Live Online

💬 Inquire on WhatsApp
Level 1: Foundation ~12 Weeks

Introduction & Direct Operations

  • • Soroban frame anatomy, upper deck (heaven) & lower deck (earth) beads
  • • Correct pencil grip technique and simultaneous bead movement
  • • Direct 1-digit and 2-digit addition and subtraction (no formulas)
  • • Introduction to Anzan (mental abacus visualization without tool)
Graduation Goal: Add/subtract 10 rows of single digits in under 20 seconds.
Level 2: Small Friends ~12 Weeks

Complementary Formulas of 5

  • • Addition small friends: +4 = +5 - 1, +3 = +5 - 2, +2 = +5 - 3, +1 = +5 - 4
  • • Subtraction small friends: -4 = -5 + 1, -3 = -5 + 2, -2 = -5 + 3, -1 = -5 + 4
  • • 2-digit sums with carrying & borrowing across columns
  • • Speed flashcard drills with vocal number dictation
Graduation Goal: Mental calculation of 2-digit 3-row numbers without pencil.
Level 3: Big Friends ~12 Weeks

Complementary Formulas of 10

  • • Base 10 addition: +9 = -1 + 10 up to +1 = -9 + 10
  • • Base 10 subtraction: -9 = -10 + 1 up to -1 = -10 + 9
  • • Complex multi-digit mixed formula calculations
  • • Audio arithmetic test: 5 rows 2-digit sums in 15 seconds
Graduation Goal: 80% accuracy in Level 3 certified grading exam.
Level 4: Multiplication ~12 Weeks

Soroban Multiplication Mastery

  • • Understanding column shifting for multiplicand and multiplier
  • • 2-digit by 1-digit, and 3-digit by 1-digit multiplication
  • • Advanced Anzan speed visualization drills
  • • Elimination of finger counting habit permanently
Graduation Goal: 50 mental sums in under 5 minutes.
Level 5: Division ~12 Weeks

Division & Long Operations

  • • Multi-digit division rules, remainder handling, quotient placement
  • • Decimal numbers introduction on Soroban
  • • High-speed 3-digit multiplication
Graduation Goal: State-level competitive exam participation eligibility.
Levels 6 to 8: Grand Master Senior Certification

Complete Mental Sovereignty

  • • Decimals, negative arithmetic, square roots and cube roots
  • • Pure Anzan (lightning mental sums without touching the board)
  • • Official Grand Master certification and national championship honors
Award: Bright Path Academy Grand Master Gold Medal & Certificate.
