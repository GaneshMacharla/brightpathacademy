Abacus & Vedic Maths

Target Age: 5 to 12 Years | Small Batches | Mode: Classroom & Live Online

🧮 Hands-on Soroban

Children learn bead values and place values on the Japanese Soroban, then add, subtract, multiply and divide with both hands.

🧠 Mental Maths (Anzan)

Step by step, children picture the abacus in their mind and solve sums mentally, building speed, accuracy and confidence.

🎯 Focus & Memory

Regular practice sharpens concentration, listening skills and memory, which help in every school subject.

Speed Mathematics

Vedic Maths Pro: The Ancient High-Speed System

Target: Class 4 and Above | 16 Core Sutras | Olympiad & Competitive Exam Edge

💬 Inquire on WhatsApp

⚡ 16 Sutras & Shortcuts

Master Ekadhikena Purvena, Nikhilam Navatashcaramam, and Urdhva-Tiryagbhyam (criss-cross multiplication) to solve 4-digit by 4-digit products in a single line.

🎯 Instant Square & Cube Roots

Find squares of numbers ending in 5, numbers near base 100/1000, and deduce cube roots of 6-digit numbers in under 3 seconds using mental observation.

🏆 Competitive Exam Dominance

Reduce exam anxiety in school maths tests and Olympiads by verifying answers in seconds with digit-sum checks.
