Soroban Abacus: 8-Level Detailed Syllabus

Target Age: 4 to 14 Years | Batch Size: Max 8 Students | Mode: Classroom Practical with Soroban Tool

💬 Inquire on WhatsApp
Level 1: Foundation ~12 Weeks

Introduction & Direct Operations

  • • Soroban frame anatomy, upper deck (heaven) & lower deck (earth) beads
  • • Correct pencil grip technique and simultaneous bead movement
  • • Direct 1-digit and 2-digit addition and subtraction (no formulas)
  • • Introduction to Anzan (mental abacus visualization without tool)
Graduation Goal: Add/subtract 10 rows of single digits in under 20 seconds.
Level 2: Small Friends ~12 Weeks

Complementary Formulas of 5

  • • Addition small friends: +4 = +5 - 1, +3 = +5 - 2, +2 = +5 - 3, +1 = +5 - 4
  • • Subtraction small friends: -4 = -5 + 1, -3 = -5 + 2, -2 = -5 + 3, -1 = -5 + 4
  • • 2-digit sums with carrying & borrowing across columns
  • • Speed flashcard drills with vocal number dictation
Graduation Goal: Mental calculation of 2-digit 3-row numbers without pencil.
Level 3: Big Friends ~12 Weeks

Complementary Formulas of 10

  • • Big friend addition formulas: +9 through +1 utilizing base 10 shifts
  • • Big friend subtraction formulas: -9 through -1
  • • Combined family formulas (5 & 10 combined operations)
  • • 3-digit number handling on Soroban board
Graduation Goal: 80% passing grade in Level 3 Board Assessment exam.
Level 4: Multiplication ~12 Weeks

Fast Mental Multiplication

  • • Introduction of Soroban multiplication rods placement
  • • 2-digit × 1-digit multiplication (e.g., 78 × 7)
  • • 3-digit × 1-digit multiplication
  • • Advanced Anzan speed drills with stopwatch timers
Graduation Goal: Solve 20 multiplication sums in under 2 minutes.
Level 5: Division ~12 Weeks

Multi-Digit Division & Quotients

  • • Soroban division methodology and quotient remainder separation
  • • 3-digit ÷ 1-digit and 4-digit ÷ 1-digit operations
  • • 2-digit × 2-digit multiplication on mental abacus
  • • Long chain mixed calculations: (45 + 78 - 32) × 4
Graduation Goal: Master long division without pencil rough-work.
Level 6: Decimals ~12 Weeks

Decimal Calculations & Precision

  • • Decimal point setting on Soroban unit rod markers
  • • Addition, subtraction, multiplication involving decimal numbers
  • • Percentage shortcuts and currency mental conversions
  • • Preparation for State Abacus Competitions
Graduation Goal: Lightning accuracy with monetary & decimal operations.
Level 7: Advanced Anzan ~12 Weeks

Square Roots & Audio Dictation

  • • Extracting square roots of 4-digit perfect squares on Soroban
  • • Pure audio flash mental calculation (student hears numbers and calculates)
  • • Negative number arithmetic and algebraic foundations
  • • Speed trial exams under strict international competition rules
Graduation Goal: Calculate sums while having a casual conversation.
Level 8: Grand Master Graduation Level

Mastery & Lifetime Accreditation

  • • Complete mental calculation independence without any tools
  • • Lightning 4-digit arithmetic, cubes, square roots in seconds
  • • Graduation Exam evaluated by senior educational committee
  • • REA Grand Master Certificate, Trophy, and National Competition entry
Award: REA Grand Master Gold Medal & Lifetime Accreditation.
