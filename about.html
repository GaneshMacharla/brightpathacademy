At REA Abacus & Tuition Point, we combine time-tested Japanese Soroban techniques with personalized school tutoring to create fearless, self-driven learners.

Our Core Philosophy

Nurturing Cognitive Brilliance Beyond Just School Grades

Located in Kakatiya Hills, Pragathi Nagar, REA Abacus and Tuition Point was established with a singular mission: to eradicate the widespread fear of mathematics among children and replace it with genuine enthusiasm and curiosity.

We believe that every child possesses innate genius. What they often lack is the tactile, visual bridge between abstract mathematical concepts and concrete understanding. Through the Soroban Abacus, numbers cease to be dry symbols on paper—they become tangible beads that children manipulate, see, and internalize mentally.

The REA Educational Guarantee

1

Strict Student-Teacher Ratio (Max 1:8)

Unlike crowded commercial tuition mills, our small batches ensure every single child receives tailored guidance, personalized pacing, and instant doubt resolution.

2

Dual-Hand Soroban Mastery
